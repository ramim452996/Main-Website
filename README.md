# KushtiaExpress Food Delivery Application

A modern single-page and multi-page Food Delivery web application tailored for **Kushtia, Bangladesh**, built with Laravel, PHP, Vanilla CSS, and JavaScript.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Local Cuisine & Delicacies (কুষ্টিয়ার ঐতিহ্যবাহী খাবার)**
   - Kushtia Famous Royal Shahi Kulfi Malai (কুষ্টিয়ার বিখ্যাত রয়্যাল শাহী কুলফি মালাই)
   - Shahi Mutton Kachi Biryani with Borhani (শাহী মাটন কাচ্চি বিরিয়ানি ও স্পেশাল বোরহানি)
   - Gorai River Shorshe Ilish (কুষ্টিয়ার গড়াই নদীর তাজা সরষে ইলিশ ভুনা)
   - Royal Beef Kala Bhuna & Butter Naan (গরুর কালা ভুনা ও বাটার নান)
   - Dahi Fuchka Platter, Shahi Mutton Haleem, and Sweet Malai Lassi.

2. **Bangladeshi Currency & Payments**
   - Denominated in Bangladeshi Taka (`৳` BDT)
   - Support for **bKash (বিকাশ)**, **Nagad (নগদ)**, and **Cash on Delivery (ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি)**

3. **Pages & Routes**
   - **Home (`/`)**: Interactive food catalog, filters, customization modal, slide-out cart, checkout.
   - **Orders & Tracking (`/order` & `/orders-bn`)**: Real-time 4-stage GPS timeline tracking, rider details, order receipts.
   - **Contact Us & Helpdesk (`/contact-us` & `/contact-bn`)**: 24/7 hotline, interactive support form, Kushtia branch hub locations, and FAQ.

4. **Themes**
   - Seamless **Dark Mode (`#0B0F19`)** and **Light Mode (`#F8FAFC`)** toggle.

---

## 🌿 Feature Branches

- **`branch-1`** (or `homepage`): Dedicated Homepage codebase.
- **`branch-2`** (or `contact-us`): Dedicated Contact Us codebase (English & Bengali).
- **`branch-3`** (or `orders`): Dedicated Orders & Live Tracking codebase (English & Bengali).
- **`main`**: Unified production branch combining all features.
